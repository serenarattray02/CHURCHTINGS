WHO WE ARE

Our Media Team

The media ministry works together to support worship, preaching, communication and the visual experience of every service. Each department contributes something essential.

Sunday Production Departments

▣

Projection

Operates FreeShow to display song lyrics, scriptures, preaching slides, announcements, videos and stage information. The operator listens carefully and follows service cues.

◉

Camera & ATEM

Frames shots, operates cameras, switches between live views and manages the video program and livestream using the Blackmagic ATEM SDI Extreme ISO.

♫

Sound

Supports clear and balanced sound for the congregation and livestream, working closely with worship leaders, speakers and the production team.

☼

Lighting

Helps create an appropriate visual atmosphere and ensures people and important service moments are properly illuminated for cameras and the room.

Creative Media Departments

Still Photography & Social Media

Capture meaningful moments, share ministry highlights and help communicate upcoming activities.

Flyer Design & Video Editing

Design event graphics and turn footage into polished videos for announcements, outreach and ministry storytelling.

Team members may support more than one area as they grow. Station assignments and training opportunities are coordinated by media leadership.
GROW YOUR SKILLS

Media Training Academy

Read practical lessons, explore how the systems work and try real activities. Training is designed to help new members progress from observing to operating independently.

Ideas for this week's media activities

Scripture Speed Round

In FreeShow, find a passage, switch its translation and preview it on the correct output.

Camera Angle Challenge

Prepare a wide, medium and close-up shot, then practice clean cuts following a director's cues.

Problem-Solving Circle

Discuss what to do when a camera feed goes black, lyrics disappear or a video doesn't play.

Upcoming event dates and times will be added when confirmed by team leadership. Activities above describe regular opportunities and suggested exercises, not scheduled events.
